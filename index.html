Connect Your Tiller Sheet

Sign in with your Google account to read your private Tiller sheet directly — no API keys, no sharing required. You just need a free OAuth Client ID (one-time setup).

2

3
Go to APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen → choose External → fill in App name & your email → Save.
4
Go to Credentials → Create Credentials → OAuth client ID → Application type: Web application.
